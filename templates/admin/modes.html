{# The Stripe mode switch. Dark, deliberately: this is the page you open at eleven at night to find out why nothing has sold since four, and the thing it has to say first is which funnels are taking money and which are not. Standalone rather than extending base.html — it is the one page here that writes, and the dark ground is the point: the dashboard is a thing you read, this is a thing you operate, and they should not be mistakable for each other at a glance on a phone. No JS. Every control is a form post, and every post names the mode it wants rather than "the other one" — so a reloaded tab re-asks for the state it already asked for instead of flipping the funnel back. #} Stripe modes
