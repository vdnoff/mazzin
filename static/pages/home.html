Find your style, one tap at a time

Last updated: 2026-08-09

Mazzin turns a two-minute swipe quiz into a personalized style report. No forms, no sign-up, no questionnaire — you just tap the rooms you like.

How it works

  1. Tap through pairs of rooms and pick the one that feels more like you. There are no wrong answers, and it takes about two minutes.
  2. We read the pattern in your choices — materials, tones, and light — and match it to a style.
  3. You get your style result for free. If you want the full breakdown — colors, materials, a shopping list, what to splurge on — you can unlock the complete report.

What's in the full report

  • Your color palette, with the exact tones your choices keep returning to
  • The five mistakes people with your style tend to make
  • Materials and combinations that work together — and the ones that don't
  • A shopping list you can actually use
  • Your style breakdown, including the secondary influence in your picks
  • Where the money is worth spending, and where it isn't
