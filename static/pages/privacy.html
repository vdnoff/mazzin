Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-10

Short version: you can take a quiz without telling us anything about yourself. We only learn your email address if you buy a report, and we learn it from our payment provider rather than asking you for it.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Mazzin operates mazzin.com and is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about anything in this document at support@mazzin.com.

2. What we collect

Quiz activity (no account, no name)

While you take a quiz, we record events so we can see how the funnel performs and where people drop off. Each event contains:

  • a randomly generated session identifier created in your browser, which is not linked to your identity and is not shared across sites;
  • which step of the quiz the event belongs to and which quiz it was;
  • the advertising attribution parameters present in the link you arrived through (for example subid, utm_source, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term), which tell us which campaign brought you here.

This is ordinary product analytics. On its own it does not identify you.

Purchase data (only if you buy)

If you buy a report, our payment provider passes us a small amount of information so we can record the sale and support you afterwards:

  • your email address;
  • your country;
  • the amount, currency, and payment and checkout identifiers;
  • the style result the purchase relates to.

We never see or store your full card number, expiry date, or security code. Those go directly to our payment provider.

Advertising measurement

We use Meta's advertising analytics — the Meta pixel in your browser and matching events sent from our server — to measure how our ads perform. It tells us which ads led to a completed quiz and which led to a sale, so we can stop paying for the ones that do neither. What is involved:

  • the pixel sets Meta's own cookies (_fbp and _fbc) and reads the click identifier (fbclid) from the link you arrived through — together these identify a browser and the ad click that sent it, not a person by name;
  • we send Meta an event when a page is viewed, when a quiz produces a result, and when checkout is opened;
  • when a purchase completes, our server sends Meta the amount, the currency, and your email address hashed with SHA-256. Hashing happens on our server: Meta receives the hash, not the address.

Your quiz answers, your style result, and the contents of your report are never sent to Meta.

3. What we do not do

  • No accounts. There is nothing to register for and no password to manage.
  • No cookies of our own. The only cookies set on this site are Meta's advertising cookies described above. Your browser keeps a session identifier in its own temporary storage so a quiz survives a page reload; it is cleared when you close the tab.
  • No selling or renting of personal data to anyone, for any purpose.
  • No profiling that produces legal effects for you, and no automated decision-making of that kind.

The advertising measurement described above is the whole of it. We do not run any other analytics or advertising technology, and if we add one we will say so here first.

4. Why we are allowed to use this data

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — to take your payment, generate your report, and deliver it to you.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — to understand how the funnel performs, to measure which campaigns work, and to keep the service secure and free of abuse. We keep this data minimal precisely so that it stays proportionate.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — to retain sale records for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — where advertising measurement requires it in your jurisdiction, and for any future technology of that kind.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, each acting as a processor on our instructions:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe handles your card details as an independent controller for its own compliance purposes and returns the limited purchase data listed above. See Stripe's own privacy policy for how it treats that information.
  • Meta — advertising measurement. Meta receives the pixel and server events described in section 2, including a hashed form of your email address on purchase, and acts as an independent controller for its own purposes. See Meta's own privacy policy for how it treats that information.
  • Our hosting and content delivery providers — they operate the servers and network the site runs on.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, or to establish or defend legal claims. Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; where that happens it is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

6. How long we keep it

  • Purchase records — kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires us to keep them. In Bulgaria this is generally several years from the end of the relevant financial year. We cannot delete these earlier, even on request, because the law requires us to hold them.
  • Quiz events — kept in aggregate for funnel analysis. They are not linked to your identity.
  • Support correspondence — kept while it is useful for handling your issue, then deleted.

7. Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the right to request access to your personal data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@mazzin.com from the address you used at checkout, or tell us enough to locate your purchase. We will respond within one month. There is no charge, and you do not need to explain why you are asking.

Please note that where a legal retention obligation applies to a sale record, we may have to keep that record until the obligation expires, even after an erasure request. We will tell you if that is the case.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority — in Bulgaria, the Commission for Personal Data Protection, or the authority in the country where you live.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, payment details never reach our servers, and access to what we do store is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfect, but the best protection here is that we hold very little in the first place.

9. Children

The Service is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be described here in plain language rather than buried.

11. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints about privacy: support@mazzin.com.
