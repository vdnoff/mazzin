About Mazzin

Last updated: 2026-08-09

Mazzin is a small digital products company. We make entertainment and self-discovery content — short, visual experiences that tell you something about your own taste.

What we make

Most style quizzes ask you to describe yourself in words. We think that is the wrong way round: people are much better at recognizing what they like than at naming it. So our quizzes show you pictures and ask you to choose. The pattern in your choices is the answer.

Our first product is a kitchen style quiz. The result is free. The full report — palette, materials, shopping list, budget guidance — is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and no account to create.

How we're honest about it

Our reports are built for entertainment and self-discovery purposes only. They are not professional interior-design, architectural, or construction advice, and we don't present them as such. If you are about to spend real money renovating, talk to a designer or a contractor — ideally bring your report along as a starting point for that conversation.

We also keep our data footprint small on purpose. No accounts, no profiles, no selling anyone's information. What we collect and why is set out plainly in our Privacy Policy.

Getting in touch

We are a small team and we read everything that comes in. If something is broken, if a purchase didn't deliver, or if you just want to tell us the result was wrong, write to support@mazzin.com.
