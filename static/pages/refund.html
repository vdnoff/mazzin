Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23

If you paid us and didn't get what you paid for, we'll fix it or refund you. You don't need to argue with us about it.

What you're buying

A Mazzin report is digital content. It is generated and delivered to you immediately after your payment is confirmed — normally within a few seconds. There is no physical product, nothing to ship, and nothing to return.

The 14-day withdrawal right

Consumers in the European Union normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. Your report is supplied immediately rather than on a physical medium, so what happens to that right depends on the checkout you went through — and the checkout tells you which one it is.

If you were shown a consent box. Ticking it records your express request for immediate delivery and your acknowledgement that you lose the withdrawal right once delivery begins — this is Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU. You are never obliged to tick it. If you would rather keep the full 14-day right, don't complete the purchase.

If you were not shown a consent box. Then we never asked you to give the right up, and you still have it. Email us within 14 days of your purchase saying you want to withdraw — no reason needed — and we will refund you in full. It works exactly like every other request on this page: same address, same 14 days, same reply. See How to ask below.

Waiving the withdrawal right does not waive anything else. If the content is faulty, incomplete, or not what we described, your statutory rights still apply in full — and so does everything below.

When we refund

We will refund you in full, without quibbling, if:

  • Your report never arrived. The payment went through but no report was delivered.
  • Something broke on our side. The report failed to generate, arrived empty or corrupted, or the link stopped working before you could read it.
  • You were charged more than once for the same report, or charged for a purchase you did not make.
  • The report was not as described on this site.

In most of these cases our first instinct will be to simply deliver the report properly — that is usually what people actually want. If you would rather have your money back, say so and we will refund you instead. Your choice, not ours.

How to ask

Email support@mazzin.com within 14 days of your purchase. Write from the address you used at checkout if you can, and tell us roughly when you bought and what went wrong. That is enough — you do not need an order number, a receipt, or a formal request.

We aim to reply within a few business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, and usually appear within five to ten business days depending on your bank.

Where a refund may not apply

If your report was delivered correctly and works as described, and you simply did not agree with the result, that is not normally a refund situation — the report is an entertainment and self-discovery product, and taste is subjective. That said, we would genuinely like to hear about it. Tell us what felt wrong and we will use it to make the quiz better; and if you think we have got something badly wrong, ask anyway. We would rather sort it out than leave you unhappy over a few euros.

Chargebacks

Please write to us before opening a dispute with your bank. A chargeback takes weeks and costs us a fee; an email usually takes a day and gets you a better outcome. We have never refused a genuine problem.
